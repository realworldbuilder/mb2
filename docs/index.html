Today's Reading List 2026-08-25

$10 billion, 9,712 jobs, and Tesla hasn't picked a state yet. That's the number on the table before a single foundation gets poured.

Tesla is scouting a $10B solar cell plant for Fort Bend County, Texas, under the name Project Crystal Sun. The site would add more than 9,712 jobs and could break ground as soon as this year — but Tesla's still shopping the deal to multiple states, so nothing's locked. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/tesla-eyes-10b-solar-cell-manufacturing-site-fort-bend-county-texas/828532/

XPeng's humanoid robot spinoff just pulled in $900M at a $6.3B valuation. Dogotix is the automaker's bet on building humanoid robots at scale — the kind of money that says someone thinks these things are leaving the demo stage for good. https://www.therobotreport.com/xpeng-motors-humanoid-robot-unit-dogotix-raises-900m/

The Port of Long Beach is stiffening the ground itself instead of just building on top of it. Engineers installed a grid system that doubles soil strength at the second-busiest container seaport in the country — the kind of unglamorous infrastructure fix that keeps a seismic zone from turning into a liquefaction problem when the ground shakes and the cranes are still standing. https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/8/4/the-grid-that-doubles-the-strength-of-the-ground

Drones are now landing on icebergs using the same microspine claws that let robots climb walls. Canadian roboticists built the system to perch on floating ice in the Arctic — a surface that moves, melts, and doesn't care about your landing gear. No word yet on what happens when the berg rolls. https://spectrum.ieee.org/arctic-iceberg-drones

China just pushed its Chang'e 7 Moon launch to 2027, saying flatly the mission "does not meet the conditions for launch." No spin, no partial fix — just a straight admission that "absolute success" wasn't there yet and the schedule lost. https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/08/due-to-need-for-absolute-success-china-delays-critical-moon-launch-to-2027/
