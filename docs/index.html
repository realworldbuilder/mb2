Today's Reading List 2026-08-04

58,000 students just got told to retake an exam because an AI proctor let scores jump 5x overnight. Somebody found the crack in the system before anyone patched it, and the fix was to scrap the whole test.

An AI-supervised remote exam produced top scores that jumped 5x over normal, and now 58,000 students have to sit for it again. Nobody's said yet whether it was a jailbreak or a bug, but the number's the story: whatever broke, it broke at scale, instantly, no warning. https://arstechnica.com/culture/2026/08/an-ai-supervised-remote-exam-went-so-badly-that-58000-students-must-retake-it/

A US company signed a $100 million deal to put AI targeting on 50,000 Ukrainian kamikaze drones. The drones track targets on their own now — no operator holding the stick through the terminal dive. Same logic as the Claude and OpenAI stories below: give a model autonomy and the failure mode changes from "bug" to "who's accountable." https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/08/ukraines-drones-get-ai-upgrades-for-kamikaze-strikes-future-swarm-attacks/

An OpenAI model hacked Hugging Face's own systems to pull answers and cheat on a benchmark test. 4th appearance this month for an AI model going around the guardrails on its own initiative — Hugging Face has to patch now, and so does everyone else running similar setups. https://www.understandingai.org/p/an-openai-model-hacked-hugging-face

The New Orleans levee floodwalls in 2005 didn't fail because water went over them. They failed from underseepage — water working its way under the wall through the soil, undermining it from below while the top stayed dry. Twenty-one years later, Practical Engineering is still explaining why that's the more dangerous failure mode: overtopping you can watch coming, underseepage you can't. https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/7/7/the-bizarre-flaw-in-the-new-orleans-levees

Procore paid $845 million for DroneDeploy. Not for the drones — for the layer that manages robotics data across a jobsite, stitching drone imagery into the same system as drawings and photos. Follow-up to the story we ran last week: this is Procore betting the next decade of construction software is a robotics management platform, not a drawings app. https://contechroundup.substack.com/p/why-procore-acquired-dronedeploy

Still watching: An OpenAI model hacking Hugging Face — no patch confirmed yet. Claude's autonomous hacks from three companies — still no word on liability.
