Today's Reading List 2026-08-20

A startup nobody's heard of just raised $1 billion to build weapons that fly faster than sound — and the guys behind it used to build rockets for SpaceX.

Castelion pulled in $1 billion for hypersonic missiles, founded by former SpaceX executives chasing a low-cost hypersonic weapon and a broader lineup of long-range strike and missile defense hardware. Same playbook as the last rocket boom: cheap, fast, iterate. Different payload. https://spacenews.com/hypersonic-missile-startup-castelion-raises-1-billion/

Geogrid can double the strength of the ground under your feet, and the Port of Long Beach is betting the second-busiest container yard in the country on it. Instead of hauling in new fill or driving piles, they're laying a grid layer that locks soil particles together and lets the existing ground carry container stacks it couldn't before. Cheaper than replacing the subgrade, and it's already under a working port. https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/8/4/the-grid-that-doubles-the-strength-of-the-ground

A drone just landed on an iceberg using claws borrowed from wall-climbing robots. Roboticists in Canada fitted microspines — the same mini-spike tech used to get robots to stick to walls and ceilings — onto drones so they can perch on Arctic ice instead of burning battery hovering over it. No word yet on what happens when the berg calves under it. https://spectrum.ieee.org/arctic-iceberg-drones

Persona AI thinks the first real job for humanoid robots isn't a warehouse or a kitchen — it's shipyard welding. The pitch: stop making YouTube demo reels and pick a task that's actually economically viable today. Welding in a shipyard is dirty, repetitive, and short on labor, which is exactly the kind of job the humanoid hype has been avoiding. https://spectrum.ieee.org/persona-ai-humanoid-robot-welding

Turner-Wohlsen JV landed a $100 million expansion at the Brandywine Conservancy & Museum of Art in Chadds Ford, Pennsylvania. The job adds a 40,000-square-foot building to house the Wyeth family's art holdings — a straight design-and-build number for a museum, not a stadium or a data center for once. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/turner-wohlsen-construction-brandywine-museum-wyeths/828327/
