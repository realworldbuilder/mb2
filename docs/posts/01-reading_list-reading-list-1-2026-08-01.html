Boston's flood plan costs $10 billion. Skip it, and the number on the other side of the ledger is $41 billion in damages — that's the trade the Army Corps just signed off on.

Boston's coastal resilience plan just got the federal green light. A $10B plan covering more than 20 flood-protection projects now qualifies for a 65% federal cost share after an Army Corps feasibility study — the study also pegs the projects as preventing $41 billion in damages if they don't get built. That's a 4-to-1 spread between the fix and the flood. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/boston-10b-coastal-resilience-infrastructure-plan-usace/826700/
