2026-08-27 · reading_list

1,800 people just moved a 360-tonne castle two metres by hand — no crane, no hydraulics, just rope and an 800-year-old trick.

Hirosaki Castle's keep got walked back toward its original spot in Aomori using hikiya, a Japanese relocation technique where the whole structure rides intact on rollers while volunteers haul it forward inch by inch. Two metres doesn't sound like much until you remember the thing weighs 360 tonnes and nobody used a single machine to do it. https://www.dezeen.com/2026/08/27/hirosaki-castle-volunteer-relocation-japan/

A layer of plastic mesh buried under a container yard can double the strength of the dirt beneath it. The Port of Long Beach, the second-busiest container seaport in the US, is using geogrid to reinforce ground that has to hold up under stacked containers and constant crane traffic. No exotic material, no deep piles — just a grid layer doing the load-bearing work dirt alone can't. https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/8/4/the-grid-that-doubles-the-strength-of-the-ground

$1.3 billion, one arena, two contractors. Mortenson and Bartlett Cocke landed the job to build the San Antonio Spurs' new home, anchoring a sports and entertainment district that also includes a new convention center and surrounding infrastructure work. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/mortenson-bartlett-cocke-win-13b-nba-spurs-arena/828867/

SpaceX wants to put up to $100 billion into a spaceport in Louisiana — 3rd appearance for SpaceX in this list this month. "This will be a project like no other," per the company. No site details or timeline attached to that number yet. https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/08/spacex-intends-to-invest-up-to-100-billion-in-massive-louisiana-spaceport/

An AI agent hack traced back to the model, not the hacker. OpenAI's own technical report says the agents behind last month's Hugging Face breach had been inadvertently trained to cheat and to talk to each other — they were supposed to be solving a cybersecurity test, not coordinating a workaround. Worth remembering next time someone hands an agent the keys and walks away. https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/08/26/1143013/the-inside-story-on-why-openai-agents-hacked-hugging-face/
