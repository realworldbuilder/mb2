Reading list 1 — 2026-08-11

2026-08-11 · reading_list

New Orleans lost a city to Katrina in 2005 not because the storm was too strong, but because someone got the levee design wrong. That's the story worth reading twice — the flood wasn't the disaster, the engineering was.

Katrina slammed into the Gulf Coast as one of the strongest hurricanes ever to hit a US coastline, and the levees still failed. Turns out the failure traces to a specific design flaw in the system, not just storm strength beyond spec. Worth knowing before you sign off on the next flood-control drawing. https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/7/7/the-bizarre-flaw-in-the-new-orleans-levees

The Port of Long Beach just doubled the strength of dirt without moving any of it. A geogrid layer under the container yard doubles ground bearing strength at the second-busiest container seaport in the country. Long Beach and its next-door neighbor Los Angeles move enough freight that "good enough" ground isn't good enough — this is the fix. https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/8/4/the-grid-that-doubles-the-strength-of-the-ground

58 welders just went cloud-connected. Tate deployed 58 Hirebotics cobot welders across multiple facilities, replacing hand welding with what they're calling an agile, cloud-connected automation network. That's not one pilot line — that's a fleet. https://www.therobotreport.com/tate-deploys-58-hirebotics-cobot-welders-across-multiple-facilities/

One valve took down a rocket. Blue Origin says the anomaly that caused its catastrophic accident originated at the main oxygen valve on one of the BE-4 engines. Single point, whole vehicle lost — the kind of failure mode every mechanical engineer already loses sleep over. https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/08/blue-origin-narrowing-in-on-root-cause-of-catastrophic-rocket-accident/

Seven months after a launch failure, Japan's H3 rocket put a navigation satellite in orbit without issue on Aug. 10. No drama in the writeup, which after last time is the whole point. https://spacenews.com/h3-launches-navigation-satellite/
