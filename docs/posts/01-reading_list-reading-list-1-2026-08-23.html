Reading list 1 — 2026-08-23

2026-08-23 · reading_list

Turner Construction, one of the largest general contractors in the country, just had a ransomware group claim it stole salary records, bank accounts, and Social Security numbers — plus engineering documents and military project files. If that claim holds, the exposure runs from payroll to national defense.

A ransomware group called Payouts King claims it accessed Turner's salary info, bank accounts, and SSNs. ClaimDEPOT, which tracks class-action filings, says the group also claims to have engineering documents and military project files. Turner disclosed the breach; no word yet on how much of the claim is verified. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/turner-construction-data-breach-ssns-bank-accounts/828454/

The ground under the second-busiest container port in the US isn't dirt anymore — it's dirt plus a grid that doubles its strength. The Port of Long Beach uses geogrid soil reinforcement to handle the load of stacked containers and heavy equipment without needing to haul in and compact way more material. Cheaper than over-excavating, and it's holding up cargo that keeps the west coast supply chain moving. https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/8/4/the-grid-that-doubles-the-strength-of-the-ground

A drone just grabbed onto a moving iceberg using the same microspines that let robots climb walls. Roboticists in Canada are testing the setup on Arctic ice — a surface that's remote, unstable, and nothing like the lab wall the tech was built for. No word yet on what happens when the ice underneath starts to melt or shift mid-grip. https://spectrum.ieee.org/arctic-iceberg-drones

Humanoid robots keep making YouTube videos instead of getting jobs. Persona AI picked shipyard welding as the one it thinks can actually pay for itself — a task with hot, repetitive, hard-to-staff work and a controlled enough environment for a robot to be useful right now instead of someday. https://spectrum.ieee.org/persona-ai-humanoid-robot-welding

A Japanese startup called Letara just raised ¥2.6B — about $16 million — pre-Series A, betting on hybrid rocket engines to undercut the cost of getting to orbit. Hybrid engines mix solid and liquid propellant, which is supposed to make them cheaper and simpler to build than the usual liquid stack. Early money, no flight numbers yet. https://payloadspace.com/letara-raises-%c2%a52-6b-to-build-its-hybrid-rocket-engine-tech/
