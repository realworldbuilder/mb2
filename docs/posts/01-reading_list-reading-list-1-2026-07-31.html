$845 million just bought 20 trillion square feet of drone and drawing data — combined, nobody's said who's going to actually look at all of it.

Procore is buying DroneDeploy for $845M in cash. The deal stitches Procore's hundreds of millions of drawings and photos to DroneDeploy's 20 trillion square feet of aerial and site imagery. That's a lot of visual data sitting in one platform — the real test is whether a superintendent gets anything useful out of it at 6 AM, or just a bigger pile to scroll through. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/procore-acquires-dronedeploy-data-ai-cash/826667/

Turner's cost benchmark jumped 5.2% quarter over quarter. Last quarter it moved 1.4%. Turner points to rising demand for skilled labor as the driver — worth knowing before you lock in a bid based on last quarter's numbers. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/turners-building-cost-index-q2-2026/826514/

Dulles is getting a $22.5 billion overhaul that kills the people movers. DOT's plan rips out the above-ground trams and replaces them with a U-shaped train. Big number, bigger question: what happens to the schedule once demolition starts on a live airport. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/trump-plans-dulles-airport-overhaul/826576/

K2 Space just raised $500 million and doubled its valuation in seven months. The Series D funds a production ramp toward 100 large spacecraft a year. That's assembly-line talk for hardware that used to get built one at a time. https://spacenews.com/k2-space-raises-500-million-for-commercial-defense-satellite-expansion/

An engineer rebuilt an obsolete IC just to keep a Betacam SP tapedeck alive. Betacam SP wasn't the home Betamax — it was the professional format broadcasters ran into the 21st century. When the chip that runs it stops existing, somebody has to make a new one from scratch. That's field manual thinking applied to magnetic tape. https://hackaday.com/2026/07/30/rebuilding-an-obsolete-ic-to-save-a-betacam-sp-tapedeck/
