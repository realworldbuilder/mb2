Reading list 1 — 2026-08-21

2026-08-21 · reading_list

A drone just landed on a moving iceberg using claws borrowed from wall-climbing robots. Nobody mentions what happens when the thing tips or the ice you're gripping starts to melt underneath you.

Landspace says it'll refly its Zhuque-3 first stage within six months of landing it. The Chinese launch firm stuck the vertical landing on Aug. 18 — first one of its kind for the company — and is already talking turnaround time instead of taking a victory lap. https://spacenews.com/landspace-aims-to-refly-recovered-zhuque-3-booster-within-six-months/

A grid buried in the dirt is doubling the strength of the ground under the second-busiest container port in the country. The Port of Long Beach is using geogrid reinforcement to stabilize soil that has to carry the weight of everything moving through the west coast supply chain. Same trick, dumber material, bigger payoff than most of what gets called innovation. https://practical.engineering/blog/2026/8/4/the-grid-that-doubles-the-strength-of-the-ground

Roboticists in Canada are landing drones on Arctic icebergs using microspines — the same mini-spike tech built for robots to climb walls and ceilings. It's one thing to grip a wall that isn't going anywhere. An iceberg drifts, melts, and rolls. No word yet on what the failure mode looks like. https://spectrum.ieee.org/arctic-iceberg-drones

Persona AI picked shipyard welding as the first real job for its humanoid robot — not a warehouse demo, not a kitchen video. The pitch: welding is one of the few tasks where a humanoid pencils out economically right now, instead of just looking good on YouTube. Worth watching whether "economically viable" survives contact with an actual hull. https://spectrum.ieee.org/persona-ai-humanoid-robot-welding

Turner-Wohlsen JV landed a $100 million expansion at the Brandywine Museum in Chadds Ford — 40,000 square feet added to a building that houses the Wyeth family's work. Straightforward museum job, no gimmicks, just a number worth knowing if you're bidding anything near Chester County this year. https://www.constructiondive.com/news/turner-wohlsen-construction-brandywine-museum-wyeths/828327/
